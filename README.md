# Simple Interest Calculator

A simple program that calculates the simple interest earned or paid on a sum of money over a period of time.

## Formula

Simple Interest (SI) = (P × R × T) / 100

- **P** = Principal amount
- **R** = Annual rate of interest (in %)
- **T** = Time period (in years)

## Inputs

| Input | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| Principal | The initial amount of money |
| Rate | Annual interest rate (%) |
| Time | Duration in years |

## Output

- Simple interest amount
- Total amount (Principal + Interest)

## Example

Principal = 10,000, Rate = 5%, Time = 2 years

SI = (10000 × 5 × 2) / 100 = **1,000**
Total amount = **11,000**

## How to Use

1. Enter the principal, rate, and time.
2. Run the calculator.
3. View the calculated interest and total amount.
